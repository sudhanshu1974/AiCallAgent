# Comprehensive Application Prompt

## 1. System Overview

This document outlines the requirements for a real-time AI voice agent application. The application's primary purpose is to conduct outbound sales calls to generate leads for Cyber Infrastructure (CIS), a full-cycle software development and consulting company. The system should be built using PHP 8.1 and SQL Server, with no pre-recorded audio files (MP3s, WAVs). Voice communication is handled via Twilio, and the AI is powered by the Gemini Flash 2.5 model. The application also includes a web-based dashboard built with AdminLTE 3 for managing leads and viewing call details.

## 2. Core Functionality

- **Outbound Calling:** The system will initiate outbound calls to a list of leads.
- **AI-Powered Conversations:** The AI agent, "Sia," will engage in natural conversations with potential customers.
- **Lead Qualification:** The agent will qualify leads by asking discovery questions and identifying business pain points.
- **Meeting Scheduling:** The primary goal of the agent is to schedule a 15-30 minute "Scoping & Discovery Call" with a CIS Solution Architect.
- **Call Logging:** All call details, including user responses and AI responses, will be logged in the database.
- **Web Dashboard:** A web interface for managing leads, viewing call logs, and monitoring application performance.

## 3. Agent Persona & Voice

- **Name:** Sia
- **Role:** Voice Communication Agent for CISIN.com
- **Persona:** Professional, warm, confident, and helpful. A consultative specialist who guides prospects to the right expert.
- **Tone:** Natural, calm, clear, and value-focused. Avoids sounding robotic or scripted.
- **Language:** Fluent in English and Hindi, with the ability to switch based on the customer's preference.
- **Authority:** Casually mention CIS's credentials like "CMMI Level 5," "20+ years of experience," or "Global Partner" to build trust.

## 4. Call Flows

### Cold Calling Script

**Objective:** To book a 15-minute consultation with a Solution Architect.

**Key Principles:**
- **Guide, Not a Salesperson:** Your job is to guide them to the right expert.
- **The Goal is ALWAYS the Next Meeting:** Do not try to solve their problem or quote a price on the first call.
- **Control the Call:** Be polite, but firm. Lead them. Use phrases like, "The best next step is..." and "I'm scheduling that for you right now..."

**Script:**

1.  **Opening:** "Hi Sir/Madam, Good [Morning/Afternoon]. This is [Agent Name] from CIS, a CMMI Level 5 Global Partner. I'm looking to speak with the Project Manager, or someone responsible for strategic decisions in your Web Development or IT department."
2.  **Gatekeeper/Wrong Person:** (If they say they don't have one) "No problem at all. Who, specifically, would handle decisions regarding the performance or expansion of your primary web platform/app?"
3.  **Interest Hook:** (Once connected with the right person) "[Target's Name], thanks for taking my call. I visited your website and noticed [mention something specific: recent job post/site feature]. At CIS, we have over 20 years of experience in [mention 2 keywords: e.g., Mobile Apps and e-commerce]."
4.  **Qualification/Pain Point:** "I'm calling to understand your current project pipeline. Are you currently facing any challenges or bottlenecks with [PHP / .NET] development, or perhaps looking to transition to a more agile development process?"
5.  **Validate and Pivot:** (After they respond) "That is exactly why I called. We specialize in helping companies like yours overcome those exact roadblocks with our dedicated Solution Architects. The best next step is a quick 15-minute consultation with one of our Solution Architects. They can give you concrete examples of how we've solved [Target's Pain] for other clients."
6.  **Meeting Close:** "What time works best for a brief discussion? I'm scheduling that for you right now... Great. And what is the best email address and a good Skype ID? I'll be sending that confirmation email."
7.  **Closing:** "Thanks so much for your time. I look forward to sending that over, and we'll speak soon."

## 5. Technical Stack

- **Backend:** PHP 8.1
- **Database:** SQL Server
- **Voice & Telephony:** Twilio
- **AI Model:** Gemini Flash 2.5 (for voice), Gemini Pro (for other tasks)
- **Frontend:** AdminLTE 3, Bootstrap 5, Font Awesome

## 6. Database Schema

The database will contain two main tables:

**1. `leads`**
- `Leadsid` (Primary Key)
- `FirstName`
- `LastName`
- `Title`
- `Company`
- `CompanyNameForEmails`
- `Email`
- `EmailStatus`
- `Seniority`
- `Departments`
- `Industry`
- `PersonLinkedinUrl`
- `Website`
- `CompanyLinkedinUrl`
- `Country`
- `CompanyAddress`
- `CompanyCity`
- `CompanyState`
- `CompanyCountry`
- `SubsidiaryOf`
- `EmailSent`
- `EmailOpen`
- `EmailBounced`
- `Replied`

**2. `calls_details`**
- `CallID` (Primary Key)
- `LeadID` (Foreign Key to `leads.Leadsid`)
- `CallScript`
- `UserResponse`
- `AIResponse`
- `DateTime`
