📞 Cold Calling Script Prompt: Cyber Infrastructure (CIS) Specialist
📝 Prompt Goal
Create a detailed cold calling script for a "Consultative Specialist" (Frank Benz) at Cyber Infrastructure (CIS). The script must adhere to the following principles, with the primary goal being to book the next meeting with a Solution Architect, not to close a sale.

Core Identity & Tone
Role: Consultative Specialist (Frank Benz).

Company: Cyber Infrastructure (CIS).
Tone: Confident, professional, and helpful.
Authority Builders: Casually drop phrases like "CMMI Level 5," "20+ years of experience," or "Global Partner."
Key Principles & Strategy
Guide, Not a Salesperson: Position yourself as a guide to the right expert.
Goal is ALWAYS the Next Meeting: Do not attempt to solve their problem or quote a price ($10/hr rate is for background context only, use it only if directly asked, or rephrase it as "highly competitive global rates"). The call ends with a scheduled meeting.
Control the Call: Use polite but firm closing phrases: "The best next step is...," "I'm scheduling a quick 15-minute intro for you right now..."
Capture Business Pain: The script must include questions designed to uncover the main business pain point for the CRM notes.
Mandatory Script Steps
Opening: Clear introduction, company name, and request for the target role (Project Manager, Web Development, IT, Sales).
Gatekeeper/Wrong Person: Handling the "We don't have one" response by rephrasing: "Who handles strategic decisions for your web design needs?"
Interest Hook: A professional and specific reason for the call (e.g., "Visited your website and noticed...").
Qualification/Pain Point: Use keywords (PHP, .NET, Mobile Apps, Digital Marketing) to highlight capabilities, but immediately pivot to asking about their challenges.
Meeting Close: Confident close to secure a 15-minute consultation with a Solution Architect, followed by securing contact details (Email, Skype) to confirm.
